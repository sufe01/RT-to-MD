**Aap bina gym jaye aur bina paise kharch kiye bhi ek behtareen body bana sakte hain.** Bohot se log ghar par hi workout karke kamaal ki fitness hasil karte hain. Iske liye aapko sirf do cheezon ki zaroorat hai: **consitency (mushaqqat)** aur **sahi diet**.Ghar par body banane ke liye aapko in teen basic cheezon par kaam karna hoga:**1\. Bodyweight Exercise (Calisthenics)**Aapko kisi machine ki zaroorat nahi hai, aapka apna jism hi aapka sabse bada weight hai. In exercises se shuruat karein:

+   **Push-ups:** Yeh aapki chest, shoulders (kandhe), aur triceps ke liye behtareen hain.
    
+   **Pull-ups / Chin-ups:** Agar ghar mein ya qareeb kisi park mein koi rod ya chhat ka hissa ho jahan aap latak sakein, toh yeh aapki back (kamar) aur biceps ko mazaid mazboot banayegi.
    
+   **Squats:** Apni taangon (legs) aur thighs ko strong banane ke liye squats lagayein.
    
+   **Planks:** Apne pet (abs) aur core ko tight karne ke liye plank karein.
    

**2\. Sasti aur Desi Diet (Protein)**Gym ke bina bhi body banane ke liye protein zaroori hai. Mehnge supplements ki jagah aap sasti desi cheezein istemal kar sakte hain:

+   **Anday (Eggs):** Rozana 2 se 3 ublay huay anday khayein (zardi ke bina ya ek zardi ke sath).
    
+   **Chane (Chickpeas):** Kale chane ya safaid chane protein ka bohot sasta aur behtareen zariya hain.
    
+   **Daalein (Lentils):** Ghar mein jo bhi daal bane, usay shauq se khayein.
    
+   **Dahi (Yogurt) aur Doodh:** Inse bhi aapko achha protein aur calcium mil jata hai.
    

**3\. Sahi Tareeqa (Progression)**Shuru mein agar aap rozana 10 push-ups lagate hain, toh har hafte usko thoda badhayein (jaise agle hafte 12 ya 15 karein). Jab tak aap muscles par zor nahi dalenge, body nahi banegi.Mujhe thoda aur batayein taake mein aapka plan bana sakoon:

+   Aapki **umar (age)** aur abhi **wazan (weight)** kitna hai?
    
+   Aapka goal kya hai—**wazan badhana (muscle gain)** ya **charbi kam karna (fat loss)**?
    

————————————————————————————————— weight 70-80kg, muscle gain karna chahta hoon ————————————————————————————————— Aapka goal **muscle gain (wazan badhana/muscles majboot karna)** hai aur aapka weight (70-80kg) iske liye ek bohot accha starting point hai.Ghar par bina paise kharch kiye muscle gain karne ke liye aapko **Heavy Homeworkout** aur **Calorie Surplus (zyada khana)** par dhyan dena hoga. Niche aapka poora schedule aur diet plan diya gaya hai:**🌟 Ghar ka Workout Plan (Hafte mein 4-5 Din)**Muscle badhane ke liye aapko har exercise ke **3 se 4 sets** lagane hain aur har set mein **8 se 12 reps** (repetitions) karne hain. Agar koi exercise aasan lage, toh use thoda mushkil banayein (jaise push-ups karte waqt peeth par koi bhari bag rakh lein).

+   **Day 1: Chest & Triceps (Seena aur Baazu)**
    
    +   **Normal Push-ups:** 3 sets (jitne lag sakein)
        
    +   **Decline Push-ups:** (Per palang ya kursi par rakh kar) 3 sets — yeh upper chest ke liye hai.
        
    +   **Diamond Push-ups:** 3 sets — yeh aapke triceps ko bada karega.
        
    +   **Chair Dips:** 3 sets — kursi ka sahara lekar triceps ke liye dips lagayein.
        
+   **Day 2: Back & Biceps (Peeth aur Dole)**
    
    +   **Pull-ups / Chin-ups:** 3-4 sets (Agar rod na ho, toh kisi pakki deewar ya darwaze ke frame ka sahara lein).
        
    +   **Door Towel Rows:** Darwaze ke handle mein towel fasa kar apni taraf kheinchein.
        
    +   **Bicep Curls (Desi):** Kisi bhari balti ya bag mein kitabein bhar kar biceps ki exercise karein.
        
+   **Day 3: Rest (Aaram)** — Muscles aaram karte waqt hi badhte hain.
    
+   **Day 4: Legs & Shoulders (Kandhe aur Taangein)**
    
    +   **Bodyweight Squats:** 4 sets (Taangon ko majboot aur bada karne ke liye).
        
    +   **Lunges:** 3 sets (Har taang par).
        
    +   **Pike Push-ups:** 3 sets — yeh aapke kandhon (shoulders) ko chauda karega.
        
+   **Day 5: Abs & Core (Pet)**
    
    +   **Planks:** 3 sets (1-1 minute ke liye hold karein).
        
    +   **Crunches:** 3 sets.
        

**🍳 Sasti aur Desi Diet (Muscle Gain ke Liye)**Muscle gain ke liye aapko ghar ka khana thoda zyada khana padega. Rozana in sasti cheezon ko apni khurak mein shamil karein:

+   **Subah ka Nashta:** 2 ya 3 ublay huay anday (Eggs) + Daliya ya Roti.
    
+   **Dopahar ka Khana:** Ghar mein jo bhi salan (daal, sabzi, ya chicken) bana ho, uske sath 2 rotiyan aur dahi khayein.
    
+   **Workout se pehle (Pre-workout):** 2 ublay huay aalu (Potatoes) ya 2 kele (Bananas). Yeh aapko workout ke liye taqat denge.
    
+   **Workout ke baad (Post-workout):** Ek muthi bheege huay kale chane (Chickpeas) aur thoda sa doodh.
    
+   **Raat ka Khana:** Roti/Chawal ke sath daal ya jo bhi paka ho. Sone se pehle ek glass doodh zaroor piyein.
    

**Sabse zaroori baat:** Har hafte exercise ko thoda mushkil banate jayein (reps badhayein ya weight ke liye bag mein zyada kitabein dalein) aur **7-8 ghante ki neend** zaroor lein.Plan shuru karne ke liye mujhe batayein:

+   Kya aapke paas ghar mein ya qareeb kisi park mein **pull-ups (latakne)** ke liye koi jagah hai?
    
+   Kya aapko **anday (eggs)** khane se koi parhez ya allergy toh nahi hai?
    

————————————————————————————————— aalu, anday, kele, chane, daal, doodh, dahi, chawal, in k ilawa bhi or cheezein batao ————————————————————————————————— In cheezon ke ilawa bhi bohot si sasti, desi aur ghar mein aasani se milne wali cheezein hain jo **muscle gain** mein bohot madad karti hain. Aap inhen badal-badal kar apni diet mein shamil kar sakte hain:**🥜 Nuts aur Seeds (Healthy Fats & Protein)**

+   **Moongphali (Peanuts):** Yeh protein aur healthy fats ka sabse sasta aur behtareen zariya hai. Rozana ek muthi moongphali khane se muscle gain mein bohot madad milti hai.
    
+   **Peanut Butter:** Agar aap ghar par moongphali ko mixer mein pees lein, toh desi peanut butter ban jayega. Isko roti ya bread par laga kar khayein.
    

**🥛 Dairy Products (Haddiyan aur Muscle Mazboot Karne Ke Liye)**

+   **Paneer (Cottage Cheese):** Aap ghar mein doodh ko phad kar aasani se paneer bana sakte hain. Is mein "Casein" protein hota hai jo muscles ko dheere dheere taqat deta hai (isay raat ko khana bohot faide-mand hai).
    
+   **Lassi / Chach (Buttermilk):** Dopahar ke waqt lassi peene se digestion (hazma) accha hota hai, jis se aap jo bhi khayenge woh sharer ko lagega.
    

**🌾 Anaaj aur Carbohydrates (Taqat ke Liye)**

+   **Gandum ka Daliya (Oats/Broken Wheat):** Yeh bohot sasta milta hai. Isay doodh mein paka kar khane se weight aur muscle gain bohot tezi se hota hai.
    
+   **Shakarkandi (Sweet Potato):** Jab iska season ho, toh aalu ki jagah shakarkandi ubaal kar khayein. Yeh bodybuilders ki pasandida cheez hai kyunki yeh muscles ko solid banati hai.
    

**🫘 Beans aur Veggies (Sasta Protein)**

+   **Lobiya (Rajma / Kidney Beans):** Lal ya safaid lobiya mein bohot zyada protein aur fiber hota hai. Isko ubaal kar ya chaat bana kar khayein.
    
+   **Soyabean Badi (Soya Chunks):** Yeh market mein bohot sasti milti hain aur in mein chicken se bhi zyada protein hota hai. Hafte mein 1-2 baar aap inki sabzi ya chawal mein daal kar kha sakte hain (rozana zyada khane se parhez karein).
